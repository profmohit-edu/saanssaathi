How much smog is your family actually breathing?

AQI is the same for everyone in a city. Your dose isn't. A child playing at dusk, a grandparent on a 6 am walk and a two-wheeler commuter breathe very different amounts. SaansSaathi estimates each person's daily dose from the live forecast and their routine, then ranks the changes that cut it most.

          AI settings

          No key needed in Chrome on a computer: SaansSaathi uses Gemini Nano, Google's AI built into Chrome. It runs on your device, so your family's data never leaves your browser.

          For the highest quality, add your own Gemini API key. Calls go straight from your browser to Google; there is no SaansSaathi server. Get a free key at Google AI Studio.
